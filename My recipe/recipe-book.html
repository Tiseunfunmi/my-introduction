Classic Fluffy Pancakes

Prep Time: 10 minutes

Cook Time: 15 minutes

Servings: 4 people

Source: Allrecipes Website

Ingredients

All-purpose flour 3 cup
White sugar 1 cup
Milk 3 cup
Melted butter 2 tablespoons

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt together.
  2. Mix the milk, egg, and melted butter in a different bowl.
  3. Pour the dry mixture in the bowl and stir until it's smooth
  4. Heat a frying pan over medium-high heat.
  5. Pour the batter onto the frying pan, turn till it's brown on both sides. Serve hot (Add preferred toppings).

Pallet grilled Salmon

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Cook Time: 1 hour

Brining & Resting Time: 8 hours

Servings: 8 people

Source: Immaculate Bites

Ingredients

Brown suger 1 cup
White sugar 1 cup
Kosher salt 1/4 cup
Ground Black Pepper 2 teaspoons
Garlic powder 2 teaspoons
Selary Seeds 2 teaspoons
Salmon filet(smoked) 2-3 pounds
Lemon 1
chopped fresh dill 1 tablespoons

Instructions

  1. Mix the brown sugar, salt, black pepper, onion powder, garlic powder, celery seeds, and white pepper in a small bowl until thoroughly combined, and set aside.
  2. Remove any small bones from the salmon that might have been missed when filleting. Put it in a shallow bowl and cover it with the dry brine. Press down gently, making sure the filets are completely covered.
  3. Let it brine in the fridge for 4-24 hours, depending on the thickness of the filet and the desired saltiness.
  4. Thoroughly rinse the brine off the salmon under cold running water to remove excess salt. Pat dry with paper towels
  5. Place it skin-side down on a rack on top of a baking sheet, and place it in the refrigerator, and let it dry for at least 4 hours (preferably overnight) until the surface is lightly tacky. That helps the smoke flavor adhere to the fish.
  6. Preheat the pellet grill to 180-225°F (80-110°C) with the lid closed for 15 minutes.
  7. Remove the salmon from the fridge, sprinkle with optional seasonings, place the filets, skin-side down, directly on the grates, and insert a meat thermometer into the center.
  8. If the smoker is at 225°F (110°C), it may take 45 minutes to 1½ hours. If you're smoking it at 180°F (80°C), it may take up to 4 hours. You'll want an internal temperature of 140-145°F (60-63°C) for tender, flaky fish.
  9. Remove it from the smoker and serve it warm with lemon wedges or chilled.

Nigerian Jollof Rice

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Cook Time: 45 minutes

Servings: 6 people

Source: Immaculate Bites Website

Ingredients

Long-grain parboiled rice 5 cups
Tomato paste 2 small can
Large Onions 2 chopped
Chicken broth 4 cups

Instructions

First, blend the fresh tomatoes, red bell peppers, and scotch bonnet peppers together until smooth, then set the mixture aside.

  1. Heat vegetable oil in a large pot and fry the sliced onions until soft and and a little brown.
  2. Add the tomato paste and fry for about 5 minutes, stirring continuously so it does not burn.
  3. Pour in the blended pepper mixture and cook down until the oil rises to the top.
  4. Add your chicken broth, salt, seasoned cubes, thyme, and curry powder. Leave everything to boil.
  5. Stir in the washed rice, cover tightly, and cook on low heat until the liquid is fully absorbed.
