Zanzibar; The Escape

Where turquoise waters meet ancient history

The Ultimate Exotic Getaway

Situated just off the coast of Tanzania, Zanzibar welcomes you with miles of white-sand beaches, vibrant coral reefs, and the intoxicating scent of spices winding through the air.

Places to Go

  • Stone Town: Ancient, coral-stone alleyways.
  • Nungwi Beach: Beautiful shores and lively tides.
  • Jozani Forest: Home to rare Red Colobus monkeys.

Things to Do

  • Take a Spice Tour: Explore aromatic local plantations.
  • Snorkel Mnemba Atoll: Swim with tropical marine life.
  • Dine at The Rock: A restaurant perched on an ocean rock.
